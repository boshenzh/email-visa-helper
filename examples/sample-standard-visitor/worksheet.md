## General published evidence menu
This is the same public menu for every reader. It is not a list of what you personally should file.
- Valid passport or travel document.
- Information about home circumstances and the permitted activity, which may include employment, study, or self-employment evidence.
- Confirmation of legal residence if applying outside the country of nationality.
- Financial documents showing access to funds and the origin of those funds.
- Invitation or activity evidence where relevant.
- If someone else pays: what support is offered, that they can provide it, the relationship, and their UK status if they are in the UK.

The guide lists these as less useful, and it does not ask you to buy travel before a decision:
- Hotel bookings
- Flight bookings unless transiting
- Credit-card statements
- Photocopies of bank cards
- Bank statements or letters issued more than 1 year before the application
- Many generic certificates, photographs, and sponsor utility bills

The supporting-documents guide lists hotel bookings and non-transit flight bookings as less useful and says a visa or entry is not guaranteed, so bookings should be made with that in mind. The processing-times page says not to book travel until there is a decision.

## Translations
Documents that are not in English or Welsh need a full translation that can be independently verified, with the translator's confirmation of accuracy, the date, their full name and signature, and contact details. An AI translation draft is not that confirmation.

## Appointment
- Apply online while outside the UK if a Standard Visitor visa is required. The earliest application is 3 months before travel.
- Each person, including a partner or child, needs their own application, fee, and appointment.
- The worksheet this demo writes is not an application, a declaration, or a payment.
- Do not collect biometrics, one-time codes, UKVI passwords, or card numbers.
- The apply page says identity is proved at a visa application centre, which may be in another country, with a photograph and fingerprints.
