# Source ledger

Accessed 2026-11-08 (Asia/Shanghai). Recheck before use. Not a permanent rule set.

- Tourism, visiting family or friends, and some short permitted activities: https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor — General information and applicant-controlled worksheets only.
- Check if you need a UK visa: https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa — Ask the applicant to use the checker and record what they say it showed, with the date. Not our result.
- Electronic travel authorisation: https://www.gov.uk/eta — Explain the published ETA pages after the applicant reports a checker result. Do not decide that they need one.
- Student visa: https://www.gov.uk/student-visa — Signpost only. Sponsor, funds, English, age, and dependant rules are not implemented.
- Skilled Worker: https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa — Signpost only. No eligibility or salary check.
- Family visa: https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa — Signpost. A family visit is not permission to live in the UK.
- Transit: https://www.gov.uk/transit-visa — Airside Direct Airside Transit versus landside Visitor in Transit, normally within 48 hours. Longer stays are generally a Standard Visitor question. Exemptions depend on circumstances. The airline can say whether the journey goes through border control.
- Marriage Visitor: https://www.gov.uk/marriage-visa — Not an ordinary Standard Visitor activity. Signpost the checker and a qualified adviser.
- Find an immigration adviser: https://www.gov.uk/find-an-immigration-adviser — Referral link for personalised advice, refusals, and complex cases.
- Immigration Rules Appendix V: Visitor: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-v-visitor — Primary rules include V 4.2 and V 4.3. This demo does not apply them to a person.
- Appendix Visitor: Visa national list: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-visitor-visa-national-list — The list exists and has exceptions. It is intentionally not copied into prompts or used as an automated verdict.
- Home Office immigration and nationality fees, 8 April 2026: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visa-regulations-revised-table/home-office-immigration-and-nationality-fees-8-april-2026 — Fee table updated 10 September 2026. Snapshot only.
- Visa processing times outside the UK: https://www.gov.uk/visa-processing-times — Updated 9 September 2026.
- Apply for a Standard Visitor visa: https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor/apply-standard-visitor-visa — Official application channel. This inbox does not submit it.

Supporting documents updated 2026-10-08: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/visitor-visa-guide-to-supporting-documents/guide-to-supporting-documents-visiting-the-uk

These statements conflict for an individual VAC. This demo does not choose between them. Escalate, and follow the instructions on the applicant's own UKVI or VAC message. Never ask for a passport to be posted to this inbox.
