# Regulatory boundary

This file is an operator note for the demo. It is not legal advice and it is not a clearance to give personalised UK immigration advice.

Accessed 2026-11-08 (Asia/Shanghai). Re-read the primary sources before any public launch. The August 2025 Immigration Advice Authority practice note was incorporated from the project research brief; this coding session did not re-download that PDF. The Home Office caseworker guidance below was fetched and read.

## What this inbox may do

- Publish general procedural information that is not applied as a personal recommendation.
- Signpost official checkers, route overviews, and the adviser finder.
- Prepare a clerical worksheet that repeats the applicant's own words, leaves blanks, and does not say those words meet the rules.

## What it must not do

- Give an individual opinion on options, eligibility, evidence strategy, prospects, or a remedy.
- Choose which evidence proves a requirement, or decide whether circumstances meet the Immigration Rules.
- Submit an application, pay a fee, make a declaration, or correspond with UKVI or a visa application centre on someone's behalf.
- Treat form filling as a blanket exemption. It is not a blanket exemption. Following an applicant's own wording can stay clerical. Choosing what to put, or what it means legally, can be regulated work.
- Claim an exemption because the assistant is software, because a server is abroad, or because a disclaimer was shown. The research brief records that the practice note's territorial examples are not a ruling on this service. Operator establishment, staff, and where the automation is run need counsel or IAA clarification before personalised functions are enabled.
- Treat ordinary human review as qualified supervision.

## Categories used in code

| Category | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `GENERAL_INFORMATION` | Official snapshots and links, not applied as the person's result. |
| `CLERICAL_APPLICANT_CONTROLLED` | Worksheet of the applicant's own statements for an ordinary visitor preparation request, with no complication flag. |
| `REGULATED_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | Personalised advice, uncertainty, refusal, minors, work, study, marriage, transit exemptions, inconsistent facts, or a VAC/passport-instruction conflict. Default when unsure. |
| `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | Not a visa-preparation request. No tools. |

Home Office caseworker guidance, read this session, says section 84 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 requires a qualified person to provide immigration advice or immigration services in the course of a business in the UK, whether paid or not. Qualified routes named there include IAA registration, an approved regulator, and work under the supervision of such a person. The guidance says doing the work without qualification is a criminal offence. That is a signpost to the statute and the guidance, not a conclusion about this operator.

Public launch and any personalised automation stay blocked until counsel approves the exact functions, the jurisdiction, and an accountable qualified supervisor. A disclaimer is not that approval.

The IAA Code of Standards was not re-read in full. The research brief notes a six-year client-file retention expectation for regulated files. This demo's 30-day TTL is only a product choice for its synthetic store. Do not promise that TTL for regulated records, and do not promise that a model provider has deleted anything.
