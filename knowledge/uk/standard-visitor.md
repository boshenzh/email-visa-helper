## Snapshot, recheck before use

- Standard Visitor visa up to 6 months: £135.
- 2 / 5 / 10 year long-term visitor visas: £506 / £903 / £1,128, normally up to 6 months per visit.
- Medical visitor up to 11 months and academic visitor up to 12 months: £234 each. Out of this demo.
- ETA: £20 per person, including children. Usually a decision within a day; allow 3 working days. Valid 2 years or until passport expiry, whichever is sooner. Not a visa and not permission to enter.
- Usual outside-UK decision time for a Standard Visitor: 3 weeks after identity and required documents, not after this inbox writes a draft.
- Direct Airside Transit visa fee snapshot £41.50. Visitor in Transit (landside) fee snapshot £74.50. Transit pages say landside transit is for leaving within 48 hours; a longer stay is generally a Standard Visitor question. Exemptions depend on the facts. This demo does not apply them.

## Identity and passport handling

The apply page says the VAC returns the passport or travel document the same day, and a successful applicant receives an eVisa. The general processing page still talks about passports left at a VAC. Do not resolve that conflict in software. Do not ask an applicant to post a passport here.

## Funds

Appendix V requires sufficient funds for reasonable costs. The supporting-documents guide, as reviewed, does not state a universal minimum balance or a universal six-month statement. It does ask for access to funds and the origin of funds. Do not invent a figure, and do not suggest hiding borrowed money.

## This demo does not decide

Nationality is not inferred from a name, language, or email address. The visa-national list is not embedded. The applicant uses the official checker and may tell us what it showed. That report is their statement, not our finding.
